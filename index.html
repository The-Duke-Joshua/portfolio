Staff e-learning platform

Healthcare group · HQ and 14 centres
Challenge
New staff needed onboarding videos and training in one private place, with no budget for paid cloud hosting.
What I did
Deployed Moodle on Oracle Cloud's free tier, organised courses by department and set up staff groups for each unit.
Outcome
A staff-only learning platform ready for course content, at no hosting cost.
Delivered

Vendor quotation analysis

Procurement · 8 states
Challenge
More than 20 supplier quotations arrived from centres in eight states, in different formats, units and tax treatments.
What I did
Built one comparison workbook that lines up every item, picks the lowest valid quote per centre and flags inconsistencies.
Outcome
Procurement could compare vendors fairly and see which quotes needed follow-up before payment.
Delivered

Scan-to-email recovery

Headquarters · office network
Challenge
The office multifunction printer stopped sending scanned documents to staff mailboxes.
What I did
Traced the fault to the mail server connection, restored the service through a secured relay and briefed staff on handling sensitive documents.
Outcome
Scanning back in service, with clear data protection guidance for staff.
Delivered

Records system feedback survey

Electronic medical records rollout
Challenge
The team needed honest feedback from staff who tested the new electronic medical records system.
What I did
Designed and built an anonymous Zoho survey on the sessions, how well the system fits each department and gaps staff noticed.
Outcome
Feedback that shapes staff training before go-live.
